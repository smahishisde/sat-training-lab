Targeted Lesson 5

Transformations & Algebra Error Traps

WHEN I SEE f(x+c) or f(x-c) → translate the inside shift carefully; THEN do a sign check before committing.

Recognition cue: Horizontal shifts run opposite the inside sign. Negative substitutions also demand parentheses.

Worked pattern

If f(x)=(x-5)², f(x+7)=((x+7)-5)²=(x+2)², so the vertex moves left 7. For P(-2), substitute (-2) everywhere before simplifying powers/signs.

Algebra or Desmos?

Algebra is usually faster; graphing is useful as a verification if the horizontal direction feels uncertain.

Common trap

Moving the graph in the same direction as the inside sign, or losing a negative sign during substitution.

5-question transfer check

Near transfer
1. If f(x)=(x-4)²+1, what is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=f(x+6)?
Different presentation
2. If g(x)=|x+3|, what is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=g(x-8)?
SAT-style trap
3. Let P(x)=2x³-x+4. What is P(-2)?
Strategy choice
4. The graph of y=f(x) has its minimum at x=7. At what x-value does y=f(x+11) have its minimum?
Far transfer
5. If q(x)=(x+2)²-5, what is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=q(2x-6)?
