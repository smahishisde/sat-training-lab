Targeted Lesson 5

Transformations & Algebra Error Traps

WHEN I SEE f(x+c) or f(x-c) → translate the inside shift carefully; THEN do a sign check before committing.

Recognition cue: Horizontal shifts feel backward: f(x-c) shifts right c; f(x+c) shifts left c.

Worked pattern

If f(x)=(x-5)², then f(x+7)=((x+7)-5)²=(x+2)². For negative substitutions, keep parentheses around the input through simplification.

Algebra or Desmos?

Algebra is usually faster; Desmos is a useful verification tool when shift direction is uncertain.

Common trap

Dropping parentheses around negative inputs or moving the graph in the same direction as the inside sign.

Transfer practice

1. If f(x)=(x-4)²+1, what is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=f(x+6)?
2. If g(x)=|x+3|, what is the x-coordinate of the vertex of y=g(x-8)?
3. Let P(x)=2x³-x+4. What is P(-2)?
