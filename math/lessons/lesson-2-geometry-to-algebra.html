Targeted Lesson 2

Turning Geometry Into Algebra

WHEN geometry fixes coordinates or lengths in terms of one variable → translate the picture into an equation before calculating.

Recognition cue: A circle tangent to both positive coordinate axes with radius r has center (r,r).

Worked pattern

If (a,b) is on that circle, use (a-r)²+(b-r)²=r². The geometry is now one algebra equation in r.

Algebra or Desmos?

Algebra is often cleaner when the question asks for a sum or product of possible radii; Vieta can avoid solving both roots.

Common trap

Using distance from the point to the origin instead of to the center.

Transfer practice

1. A circle tangent to both positive axes passes through (6,8). The possible radii have sum S. Find S.
2. A circle tangent to both positive axes passes through (4,7). The possible radii have product P. Find P.
3. A circle tangent to both positive axes passes through (5,5). Enter the smaller radius as 10-5sqrt(2).
Transfer complete. Record anything that still felt unfamiliar.
