Targeted Lesson 3

Hidden Similarity in Right Triangles

WHEN I SEE an altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse → expect three similar triangles.

Recognition cue: The altitude splits the hypotenuse into two segments and creates three similar right triangles.

Worked pattern

If the two hypotenuse segments are p and q, the altitude h satisfies h²=pq. Each leg also has a geometric-mean relationship with the full hypotenuse.

Algebra or Desmos?

Usually algebra and similarity are faster than Desmos.

Common trap

Finding the altitude correctly but answering with it when the question asks for area or a leg.

Transfer practice

1. The altitude to a right triangle's hypotenuse divides it into segments 4 and 9. Find the altitude.
2. The altitude divides the hypotenuse into 5 and 20. Find the area of the original triangle.
3. A right triangle has hypotenuse 25. One hypotenuse segment adjacent to a leg is 9. Find that leg.
