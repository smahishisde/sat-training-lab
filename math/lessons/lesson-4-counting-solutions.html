Targeted Lesson 4

Counting Solutions & Intersections

WHEN I SEE 'how many solutions' with an absolute value or parameter → think intersections before solving individual equations.

Recognition cue: A parameter k often represents the height of a horizontal line. Count intersections, not algebra branches.

Worked pattern

For |x²-9|=k, y=|x²-9| and y=k normally meet four times for 0

Algebra or Desmos?

Excellent Desmos use case: graph both sides and count distinct intersections.

Common trap

Solving only one absolute-value branch or counting the same x-value twice.

Transfer practice

1. For what positive k does |x²-16|=k have exactly three distinct real solutions?
2. How many distinct real solutions does |x²-25|=9 have?
3. For what positive k does |(x-2)²-36|=k have exactly three distinct real solutions?
Transfer complete. Record anything that still felt unfamiliar.
