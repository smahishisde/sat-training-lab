Targeted Lesson 4

Counting Solutions & Intersections

WHEN I SEE 'how many solutions' with an absolute value or parameter → think intersections before solving individual equations.

Recognition cue: A parameter k can be viewed as a horizontal line y=k. The solution count is the number of distinct intersections.

Worked pattern

For |x²-9|=k, there are normally four intersections for 0

Algebra or Desmos?

Excellent use case. Graph both sides and count distinct intersections, then understand why the special parameter changes the count.

Common trap

Counting algebra branches rather than distinct x-values.

5-question transfer check

Near transfer
1. For what positive k does |x²-16|=k have exactly three distinct real solutions?
Different presentation
2. How many distinct real solutions does |x²-25|=9 have?
SAT-style trap
3. For what positive k does |(x-2)²-36|=k have exactly three distinct real solutions?
Strategy choice
4. How many distinct real solutions does |x²-4|=4 have?
Far transfer
5. For what positive k does |4x²-100|=k have exactly three distinct real solutions?
