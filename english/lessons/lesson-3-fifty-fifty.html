English Targeted Lesson 3

The 50/50 Decision Rule: Find the Defect

WHEN two answers seem plausible → stop asking which sounds better; identify the exact textual or logical defect in one of them.

Recognition cue: Hard SAT English distractors are often 'almost right.' Look for overstatement, scope shift, unsupported causation, wrong relationship, or a detail the passage never says.

Worked pattern

If one answer says 'may contribute' and another says 'is the sole cause,' the stronger wording requires stronger evidence. When the passage is limited, the qualified answer usually fits better.

Common trap

Rereading the whole passage repeatedly without testing each answer against a specific claim.

Strategy choice
