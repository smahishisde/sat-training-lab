English Targeted Lesson 2

Inference: Supported vs. Merely Possible

WHEN an inference answer requires me to invent one extra fact the passage never established → eliminate it.

Recognition cue: The best SAT inference is the one most tightly constrained by the text. A plausible story is not enough.

Worked pattern

If older speakers rarely used a pronunciation in 1995 but commonly use it in 2025, the supported inference is that use has spread across age groups. It is NOT supported that the exact same people were recorded both times unless the passage says so.

Common trap

Choosing an explanation that could be true but depends on an unstated assumption.

Near transfer

1. A lake's water clarity improved after phosphorus inputs declined, while rainfall and lake level remained roughly unchanged. Which inference is best supported?

Different presentation

2. In 2000, very few employees over age 55 used remote-work software. In 2025, most employees over age 55 at the same company use it regularly, and younger employees continue to use it at high rates. Which inference is best supported?

SAT-style trap

3. A plant species flowers earlier in warmer years than in cooler years. Which conclusion is best supported by this observation alone?

Strategy choice

4. A study finds that students who voluntarily attend tutoring score higher than students who do not. Which statement is the safest inference?

Far transfer

5. After a city opens a new rail line, traffic on one nearby highway falls 8%, but gasoline prices also rise sharply during the same month. Which conclusion is best supported?
