English Targeted Lesson 1

Words in Context: Let the Passage Define the Word

WHEN a word is followed by an explanation, example, contrast, or consequence → predict the meaning from context BEFORE looking at the choices.

Recognition cue: The SAT often tests a familiar-looking word in an unfamiliar sense. The surrounding sentence usually gives enough evidence to define the word precisely.

Worked pattern

If a passage says evidence is 'tentative' because it appears in only two data sets and has not been replicated, predict 'not yet firm / provisional' before looking at answer choices. This prevents a familiar but wrong everyday meaning from pulling you away.

Common trap

Choosing a dictionary meaning that is possible in general but not supported by the sentence.
